# Quick Reference Sheet for Terms
---
## Foundations
  1. **Computer Vision(CV)**- The act of giving a computer "eyes" to sense the world, it involves both sensing with hardware and perceving with AI and ML
  2. **Object Detection**- A specific task in CV, where the model is suppoed to classify and identify the postion of an object in a frame or image
  3. **Ground Truths**- The "correct answer" when it comes to computer vision, usually an object
  4. **Bounding Box**- What is defined as the set or object in the image, consisting of a rectaingle and class
  5. **YOLO**- meaning You Only Look Once, an base model developed by ultralytics, if you want to learn more about YOLO read the wiki and check it out here (https://docs.ultralytics.com/#yolo-a-brief-history})

## Performance Metrics
  1. **IoU**- means intersection over union is related to the amount of overlap between the perdicition and ground truth
  2. **Percision**- Measures what amount of perdictions were correct, of the perdictions made
  3. **Recall**- Mesaures how many perdctions were made compared to the total amount of perdictions to be made ideally
  4. **Average Percision(AP)**- The average percision over multiple intervals of recall
  5. **mAP50/mAP50-95**- mean average percison is the average of the average percisions of all classes under multiple IoU, mAP50 and mAP50-95 are different tolerance levels for the IoU
  6. **FPS**- Frames per Second, related to model speed
